The Tier Ladder

No sales pressure. No donation quotas. Just pure street credit and community impact.

Tier 1: Street Credit Ambassador (0–499 XP)

Unlocks: Unique mobile tracking link, digital QR code routing, profile achievement badge.

Activity Assignment: Spread localized digital awareness and connect with anglers at local boat ramps.

🔒 Locked (500 XP)

Tier 2: Outreach Ambassador

Unlocks: Physical material deployment center (printable QR handout cards, flyers, and sticker kits).

Activity Assignment: Setting up physical outreach hubs at key bait shops and local launch priorities.

💼 143 Marine Invitation (1,000 XP)

Tier 3: Elite Field Agent

Unlocks: Regional seasonal windows, local tournament activation rights, and a formal invitation to join the 143 Marine commercial co-branded revenue program.

Activity Assignment: Staffing major tournament bag drops and local live conservation activations.

⚓ Executive Commission (2,500 XP)

Tier 4: Regional Area Leader

Unlocks: Complete map engine control tiers, localized team leadership privileges, and authorized regional revenue cuts on merch and ad-space partnerships.

Activity Assignment: Coordinating high-traffic tournament weekend field operations and team placement schedules.
